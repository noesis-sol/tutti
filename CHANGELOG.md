- A finished ticket's rebase that stops on conflicts is handed back to its worker (§ 4.7, § 5 item 7, `src/run/handback.ts`) when hand-backs are on, a check command is set, it has attempts left (2 a run) and its worker is idle or failed and not steered, or its session was closed by tutti, which is resumed for it: the rebase is left in progress, the ticket moves `Merging` → `Resolving{attempt}` with the log's `RESOLVING`, orchestra's instructions are sent `client_composed`, and the merge queue goes on meanwhile. Once the send is no longer outstanding and the worker has settled, within `merge.resolve_timeout`, tutti verifies what it left as orchestra does (the rebase finished, the worktree clean and on its branch, only the ticket's own commits on top of the base) and runs the check: `ToMerge` when it passes; else `Blocked` (`MERGE_CONFLICT`), the branch put back as the ticket closed it and its notes saying why. A conflict not handed back says why in its `MERGE_CONFLICT` line; for a steered worker the hand-back waits in the queue's `handBacks`. `Git.resetBranch` (`git reset --hard`) is new.
- A check that fails on a finished ticket's rebased branch, after a clean rebase or a resolution, is handed back to its worker to fix (§ 4.7, § 5 item 7, `src/run/checkback.ts`) when it can take it: `merge.check_hand_backs` (2) on and attempts left of it, the check finished, the run not winding down, and its worker idle or failed and not steered, or its session resumed. The ticket moves `Merging` (or `Resolving`) → `Fixing{attempt, afterResolution}` with the log's `FIXING`, orchestra's instructions (what failed, the lines that say so, what landed on the base) are sent `client_composed`, and the merge queue goes on meanwhile. tutti verifies the fix (new commits naming the ticket on top of where the check failed; the worktree clean and on its branch), sets it up if need be and checks it again: `ToMerge` when it passes, `Fixing` again with attempts left; else, as when a fix is rejected (the branch reset to where the check failed), `Review` with `CHECKS_FAILED: … after it was handed back to its worker to fix twice (see its notes)`, or `Blocked` (`MERGE_CONFLICT`) after a resolution, the branch put back as the ticket closed it. Each pass through `Merging` counts toward the attempts; a failure not handed back says why in its `CHECKS_FAILED` line.
- A ticket set aside as its check failed (`CHECKS_FAILED`) is checked once more after the base moves on (§ 5 item 8, `src/run/recheck.ts`): the merge queue keeps it, and after each merge puts it back in line, `Review` → `ToMerge` with the log's `RECHECK`, unless its check failed in a folder its own commits change, it has been checked again already in this run, or something touched it since (reopened, its worktree changed, its branch moved, its worker steered), which the log says. The `CHECKS_FAILED` line says which (`checked once more if main moves on in this run`, `not checked again in this run, as it failed in …`), and a second failure `fails again`. A ticket left for review (`src/run/aside.ts`'s `leaveForReview`) now has the log's line in its notes beside its `warn` record with `aside`.

A parent waits until its subtickets are closed and merged (`parent`); a ticket waits for a blocker, and a follow-up linked `discovered-from` for its source, until that one merges (`merge`); with a slot free, a ticket whose footprint overlaps a running ticket's, its worktree's changes included, is skipped for the next (`overlap`); a ticket labelled `solo` runs alone, dispatched as `dispatching solo`, and first in line waits for the running tickets while holding back those behind it (`solo`). The log says why once per reason (`<id> waits: …`, `skipping <id>: touches …, like running …`, the solo lines), two running worktrees changing one file give `LIKELY_CONFLICT` once, and `queue` and `dispatch` records carry `held`, `held_by` and `solo`. `src/run/holds.ts` holds the reasons; `footprintsOf` reads footprints from the checkout. `test/held-back.test.ts` covers each reason under TestClock, with a fast-check property that overlapping tickets never run side by side.
